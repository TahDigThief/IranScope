# The execution of wounded protesters inside Iranian hospitals by the islamic republic

During the January 2026 crackdown, the islamic republic security forces killed protesters in the streets, but they also systematically entered medical facilities to execute wounded civilians who were already under treatment, turning hospitals into extrajudicial killing sites. This was not collateral damage or rogue violence but a coordinated operational tactic documented by multiple independent sources, revealing a regime willing to violate the most fundamental norms of medical neutrality to erase evidence and terrorize survivors.

## Verified accounts documenting this specific atrocity

- Amnesty International verified that security forces conducted sweeping mass arrests directly from hospitals and subjected detainees to enforced disappearance. Furthermore, they documented that authorities subjected detainees to torture and ill-treatment, including the "deliberate denial of adequate food, water and medical care", actively obstructing care to conceal their crimes. ([source](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/campaigns/2026/01/what-happened-at-the-protests-in-iran/))
- Iran International reviewed classified documents and obtained testimony from three doctors and four nurses in Tehran who confirmed security forces entered hospitals to abduct and execute wounded patients undergoing treatment. They published images of corpses with gunshot wounds to the head still attached to IV tubes, syringes, and cardiac monitoring electrodes. In another instance of medical neutrality violations, nurses testified that a security agent entered an ambulance and executed a semi-conscious, severely beaten wounded young man with two consecutive shots. ([source](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202601255198))
- Euronews reported on published images showing victims with medical equipment (including IV tubes, syringes, and bandages) still attached to their bodies alongside visible gunshot wounds, providing direct evidence that injured individuals were killed while receiving hospital treatment. They also noted that medical personnel are facing "systematic elimination" for treating injured protesters and refusing to cooperate with security officers. ([source](https://www.euronews.com/2026/01/27/iran-protests-death-toll-could-surpass-more-than-30000-reports-claim))
