# The islamic republic lured a journalist out of exile with a fake meeting, then hanged him

## TL;DR

- Zam was tricked into leaving the safety of exile in France by being told he would meet a revered religious figure in Iraq.
- The IRGC kidnapped him and smuggled him back into Iran, despite Iraq's extradition agreement technically exempting political cases.
- He was tried in secret, denied a lawyer of his choosing, and convicted based on a coerced televised confession.
- He was hanged in December 2020 for running a news channel that reported on protests and government corruption.
- Governments across Europe condemned the execution as a judicial murder and cancelled diplomatic engagements in response.

Ruhollah Zam ran a Telegram channel called Amad News from exile in Paris. It grew to more than a million followers and became a key source of information during Iran's 2017-2018 protests, sharing details on when and where demonstrations would happen along with material exposing official corruption. The islamic republic saw him as a serious threat.

## Findings

- In 2019 the IRGC set a trap. Zam traveled to Iraq believing he would meet the senior Shiite cleric Ayatollah Sistani, but he had been lured into a trap, and the IRGC then oversaw his transfer back to Iran. Other reporting says a female intelligence agent from the islamic regime persuaded him to make the trip, and a French newspaper reported he had been promised a meeting with Sistani. His own colleague at Amad News was reportedly used to help set up the trip, though she denies this and says she warned him not to go.
- Once back in Iran, Zam disappeared into the system. His trial began in February 2020, presided over by judge Abolqasem Salavati, known among dissidents as the "hanging judge". The proceedings were secret, and he was not allowed to choose his own defense lawyer. He was sentenced to death on charges including spying for Israeli and French intelligence, collaborating with the United States, and gathering classified information.
- Before the verdict, state television aired a forced confession. The interview was recorded in Evin prison's Ward 2-A, which belongs to the IRGC. In it, Zam said that from day one he had been told he would receive the death sentence. His father said his son was not in good mental health during the trial, was not allowed to contact his wife and daughter, and that his interrogators pressured him further.
- On December 12, 2020, the islamic republic hanged him. He was executed for "corruption on earth," a charge officials said applied because his channel had helped incite the 2017-2018 protests.
- The international reaction was swift. France's foreign ministry called it a barbaric and unacceptable act that violated the country's international commitments, and ambassadors from France, Germany, Austria and Italy pulled out of a business forum with Iran in protest. The International Bar Association's Human Rights Institute described the execution as a judicial murder. Amnesty International and Reporters Without Borders condemned it as well.
