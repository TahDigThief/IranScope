# The islamic republic shot down a civilian airliner, lied for three days, then bulldozed the evidence

On January 8, 2020, Ukraine International Airlines Flight PS752 took off from Tehran. Minutes later it was hit by two missiles fired by the IRGC. All 176 people on board died, including dozens of Canadians and many Iranians.

## Findings

- For three days straight, regime officials denied any military involvement and pointed to a technical malfunction, even as satellite and video evidence mounted against them. Only after the evidence became undeniable did then-president Rouhani admit the military had shot the plane down. ([source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/audio-recording-iran-minister-foreign-affairs-flight-ps752-1.5906538))
- Canada obtained a secret recording, believed to be Iran's own foreign minister Zarif, suggesting the downing may have been intentional rather than a simple accident, exactly the opposite of the regime's public line. Iran called the recording fake. ([source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/audio-recording-iran-minister-foreign-affairs-flight-ps752-1.5906538))
- Canada's own forensic investigation found that the islamic republic put anti-aircraft systems on high alert right next to a civilian airport, without closing the airspace or warning any airlines, putting every single plane that took off that night at risk. ([source](https://international.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/corporate/reports/flight-ps752/factual-analysis))
- The same investigation said the regime's official story was disingenuous, misleading, and superficial, and intentionally ignores key factors, and that the blame cannot be conveniently pinned on a few junior soldiers. ([source](https://international.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/corporate/reports/flight-ps752/factual-analysis))
- Canada's report says the islamic republic bulldozed the crash site before investigators ever got there. ([source](https://international.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/corporate/reports/flight-ps752/factual-analysis))
- When the regime finally released its official report over a year later, it blamed the missile strike on a "105-degree miscalibration" and bad communication, conveniently keeping the blame away from senior commanders. The report never explained the inconsistencies that independent observers, including a UN special rapporteur, had already raised. ([source](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/04/20/iran-court-issues-sentences-downing-ukraine-flight-ps752))
- Canada's foreign minister rejected the "human error" excuse outright, saying there is no circumstance where a civilian plane gets shot down by accident in this day and age. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_International_Airlines_Flight_752))
- The regime authorities also harassed and intimidated victims' families, interfering with burials and memorials and pressuring them to accept the government's "martyrdom" label for their loved ones, while publishing photos and videos of services without permission. ([source](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/04/20/iran-court-issues-sentences-downing-ukraine-flight-ps752))
- In 2023 a military court handed down sentences in a trial with zero transparency. The lead commander got 13 years, only 10 enforceable, while the rest of the defendants got one to three years each. ([source](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/04/20/iran-court-issues-sentences-downing-ukraine-flight-ps752))
- The four affected countries called the trial a sham, saying neither the trial nor the verdicts brought any truth or justice to the families, since the whole process lacked impartiality and transparency from the very start. ([source](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/04/20/iran-court-issues-sentences-downing-ukraine-flight-ps752))
- In March 2025, the islamic republic tried to argue that the ICAO had no authority to even hear the case brought against it by Canada, Sweden, Ukraine, and the UK. The ICAO rejected that objection and is moving ahead with the case. ([source](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202503181811))
- The regime didn't stop there. In April 2025, five years after the shootdown, it sued at the ICJ trying to get that ICAO ruling thrown out entirely. ([source](https://simpleflying.com/iran-sues-ukraine-erroneously-shot-down/))
