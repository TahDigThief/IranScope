# Propaganda by the islamic republic, documented

The islamic republic runs a sophisticated online network to share propaganda, threaten dissidents, manipulate international narratives, and effectively ensure its own survival. The regime spends hundreds of millions of dollars on a massive state media empire, deploys IRGC troll armies to dox and harass activists, uses AI to fabricate reality, and actively threatens the families of diaspora dissidents back home to force them into silence. They even hand down death sentences in absentia and hijack foreign broadcasts to spread their lies.

## Sources documenting the scale and tactics of the islamic republic's digital and psychological operations

- The U.S. Department of Justice (March 2026) seized domains used by Iran's Ministry of Intelligence (MOIS) to dox dissidents, send death threats, and incite violence against journalists and regime critics living abroad: https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-disrupts-iranian-cyber-enabled-psychological-operations
- The UK Government (April 2025) published an official assessment confirming Iran actively monitors and targets diaspora dissidents online, utilizing cyberattacks, threatening critics' family members inside Iran, and handing down death sentences in absentia for online activities: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/iran-country-policy-and-information-notes/country-policy-and-information-note-social-media-surveillance-and-sur-place-activities-iran-april-2025-accessible
- The UK Parliament documented how the IRGC's Seraj Cyberspace Organisation recruits and mobilizes pro-regime users to conduct coordinated cyber operations, including trolling campaigns, spreading disinformation, artificially trending hashtags, and fomenting divisions among opposition groups: https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/138378/pdf/
- Freedom House's Freedom on the Net 2024 report extensively details the regime's "cyber army" and its systematic use of inauthentic networks to spread disinformation and target critics globally: https://freedomhouse.org/country/iran/freedom-net/2024
- PolitiFact (March 2026) highlighted how the IR has escalated its psychological operations, noting that in December 2023 an Iranian cyber group hijacked streaming services in the UAE, Canada, and the UK with a broadcast featuring an AI-generated anchor, a tactic that has only accelerated in 2026: https://www.politifact.com/article/2026/mar/23/iran-disinformation-campaigns-influence-operations/
- The International Centre for Counter-Terrorism (ICT) published an analysis of the islamic republic's information warfare during the Dec 2025-Jan 2026 protests, demonstrating how narrative control and foreign influence operations function as core strategic tools for regime survival, not just mere propaganda: https://ict.org.il/irans-information-warfare-during-the-december-2025-january-2026-protests/
- The Foundation for Defense of Democracies (FDD) noted that to fuel this global apparatus, the islamic republic tripled the annual budget of its state broadcaster (IRIB) for 2025 to a massive $480 million, funding global propaganda operations in 32 languages across multiple international TV channels: https://www.fdd.org/analysis/2025/06/18/10-things-to-know-about-tehrans-propaganda-network-the-islamic-republic-of-iran-broadcasting/
