# Iranians overwhelmingly reject the islamic republic

Independent survey data, including research from inside Iran, shows the Iranian public is overwhelmingly opposed to the islamic republic and increasingly favors regime change over reform. Due to the risks of independent polling, organizations use secure methods like VPN-based sampling, while leaked internal regime data further confirms the public's true sentiments.

## Key findings

- GAMAAN surveyed over 77,000 respondents inside Iran in June 2024, finding that 70% opposed the continuation of the islamic republic (rising to 81% during the "Woman, Life, Freedom" uprising), with support for the supreme leader falling to just 11%: https://gamaan.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Iranians-Political-Preferences-in-2024-GAMAAN-Report-English.pdf
- A follow-up GAMAAN survey in September 2025 gathered over 30,000 responses via VPN, revealing that 70-80% of Iranians across all demographics would not vote for the islamic republic, with support for regime change remaining the most popular position: https://theconversation.com/iran-protests-2026-our-surveys-show-iranians-agree-more-on-regime-change-than-what-might-come-next-273198
- A leaked internal poll from Iran's Ministry of Culture and Islamic Guidance found that 72.9% of Iranians favor the separation of religion and state: https://agsi.org/analysis/official-government-poll-72-9-of-iranians-favor-separation-of-religion-and-state/
